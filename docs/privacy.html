Privacy Policy

Illustrator Text Export and Import · Version 5.5 · Adobe Plugin ID 205341

Last updated: May 2026

Summary

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. All file operations run locally on your computer inside Adobe Illustrator. No analytics, advertising, or cloud services are used by the extension.

Information we do not collect

The CEP panel does not:

  • Collect names, email addresses, or account identifiers
  • Track usage or behavior
  • Send document content or exported XML/XLIFF to any server
  • Use cookies or local storage for profiling
  • Access the internet for data collection purposes

Local processing only

When you export or import text, the extension reads and writes files on your machine — Illustrator documents and XML/XLIFF files you choose. You control where those files are saved and who receives them.

Purchases and payments

If you buy the full version through Adobe Exchange, payment and account data are handled by Adobe under Adobe’s privacy policy — not by this extension. If you purchase via PayPal on our website, payment data is handled by PayPal.
